# Day 6 – SQL Analysis Complete

**Dataset:** Superstore (deduplicated)
**Total Rows:** 4,922 unique orders
**Total Sales:** $1,998,309.81

**Key Findings:**
- Technology is the top Category ($800,229.54)
- Phones are the top Sub-Category ($322,176.36)
- Fasteners are the worst Sub-Category ($8,670.11)

**Business Recommendations:**
- Double down on Technology and Phones
- Investigate why Furniture underperforms
- Consider discontinuing Fasteners

**Lesson Learned:**
- SQL is 10x faster than Excel for large data
- Data cleaning is 80% of the job
- Always verify your numbers
