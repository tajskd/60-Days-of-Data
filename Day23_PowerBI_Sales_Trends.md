# Day 23 – Power BI: Sales Trends Page

## Objective
To build a dedicated page in Power BI that visualizes sales trends over time using line charts.

## What I Built
I created a Sales Trends page that displays:
- **Line Chart:** Monthly sales trend with Total Sales (blue) and Average Order Value (red).
- **Second Line Chart:** Sales trend for the last 12 months (filtered to show recent performance).

## Key Learnings
1. **Line Chart:** Learned to use line charts for time-series data.
2. **Multiple Measures:** Added both Sum and Average of Sales on the same chart.
3. **Date Filtering:** Used a relative date filter to show the last 12 months.

## Key Business Insight
- **Spike in Late October:** There is a clear sales spike in late October, which aligns with the Q4 seasonal trend identified in the Python analysis.
- **Average Order Value:** Remains relatively stable throughout the year, suggesting the Q4 spike is driven by **volume** (more orders), not higher prices.

## Recommended Actions
1. **Prepare Inventory:** Increase stock levels in September to prepare for the October–November surge.
2. **Marketing Campaign:** Launch a targeted marketing campaign in September to maximize the Q4 opportunity.
3. **Staffing:** Ensure adequate support (warehouse, customer service) during the peak period.
