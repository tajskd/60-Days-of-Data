# Day 25 – Power BI: Final Polish, Adaptation & Publishing

## Objective
To finalize the dashboard design, adapt to a technical limitation (disabled map visuals), and publish the complete report to the Power BI Service.

## Project Overview
I built a complete, interactive, three-page Power BI dashboard using the Superstore dataset. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of sales performance, regional trends, and product insights.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Summary
- **4 KPI Cards:** Total Sales ($2.26M), Total Orders (4,922), Total Customers (793), Total Regions (4).
- **Top 5 Products Chart:** Bar chart highlighting the Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier as the top performer ($61,599.82).
- **Slicers:** Region and Category filters synced across all pages.

### 2. Regional Performance
- **Sum of Sales by State:** Bar chart showing state-level sales (California leads).
- **Sum of Sales by Region:** Bar chart confirming the West as the top region ($710k).
- **Average of Sales by Region:** Column chart showing the South with the highest average order value ($243).

### 3. Sales Trends
- **Line Chart:** Monthly sales trend with Total Sales and Average Order Value, revealing a clear Q4 spike (October–December).
- **Last 12 Months Filter:** Focused view of recent performance.

## Key Business Insights
1. **West Region Dominates:** Highest total sales ($710k) and customer count (681).
2. **South Region Opportunity:** Highest average order value ($243) but lowest total sales—indicating a need for customer acquisition, not higher prices.
3. **Q4 Seasonal Spike:** Significant sales increase in October–December, suggesting a need for increased inventory and staffing in Q4.
4. **Top Product:** The Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier is the clear revenue driver, supporting a B2B premium product strategy.

## Technical Adaptation
During publication, the Power BI Service blocked map visuals due to the free/school account restrictions. I **adapted** by replacing the map with a **State-level bar chart**. This maintains the same level of insight while ensuring the dashboard remains fully functional and professional.

1. **Adaptability:** When a tool blocks a feature, find another way to tell the same story.
2. **Publishing:** Power BI Service allows for easy sharing and collaboration.
3. **Design:** A consistent theme, clean layout, and aligned visuals create a professional look.
