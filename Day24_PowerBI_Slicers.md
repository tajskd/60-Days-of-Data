# Day 24 – Power BI: Adding Slicers for Interactivity

## Objective
To add slicers (filters) to the dashboard that allow users to interact with all pages simultaneously.

## What I Built
I added two slicers to the Executive Summary page:
1. **Region Slicer:** Filters all pages by region (West, East, Central, South).
2. **Category Slicer:** Filters all pages by product category (Furniture, Office Supplies, Technology).

## How I Connected Them
- Used the **"Sync slicers"** feature in the View tab to connect the slicers to all three pages.
- Now, selecting a region or category updates every chart on every page instantly.
